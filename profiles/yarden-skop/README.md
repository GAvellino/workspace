# Yarden Skop

**Posdoc Researcher** - Paderborn University, Paderborn, Germany

## About
Yarden is a Research Associate and Post-Doctoral Researcher at Paderborn University, Germany, where she works with Prof. Marcus Burkhardt in sub-project A07 "Personal Data Industries," as part of the Collaborative Research Center (CRC) 1187 "Media of Cooperation" at Siegen University.
She recently completed her PhD in Media Studies and Science and Technology Studies at the International Collaborative Research Center "Media of Cooperation" (funded by the DFG, Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft) at Siegen University. Her doctoral research conducted critical qualitative research on the development and deployment of machine learning and AI tools for the identification of toxic speech and fact-checking, approached from a platform studies and content moderation studies perspective.

She holds a Research Master's in New Media from the University of Amsterdam. Before moving to academia, Yarden worked as a news and investigations journalist, as both editor and correspondent, for more than a decade, most notably for Haaretz. Passionate about diversity and equality, she has volunteered in feminist grassroots media organizations that give women an opportunity to raise their voices.
