# Prof. Dr. Paula Helm

**Group Lead** — Empirical Ethics of the Computational
Center for Critical Computational Studies (C³S), Goethe University Frankfurt am Main

## About
Paula Helm is a Professor for Empirical Ethics of the Computational at the C3S.
She specialises in Critical Computational Studies and Empirical Ethics. Originally trained in Anthropology and Peace and Conflict Research, her work is situated at the intersection of Science and Technology Studies, Ethnographic Methods, and Technology Ethics. Her focus areas include Cultural Diversity, Critical Data Studies, Privacy & Anonymity, Platform Economy, Language Technology, and Addictions. Apart from working ethnographically, Paula likes to experiment with Computational Methods and Value Sensitive Design. Before joining the C3S, Paula was Assistant Professor of Ethics and Data Science at the University of Amsterdam, where she also served as Scientific Lead of the Empirical Ethics Research Group at the Amsterdam Institute for Advanced Studies and coordinated a new MA track in Cultural Data and AI that integrates critical inquiry with data science. The larger mission of Paula’s work is to ground value considerations in reflexive empirical inquiry and to move ethical concerns from the PR- to the Engineering-, Infrastructural- and Community-Level.
