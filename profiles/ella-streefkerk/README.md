# Ella Streefkerk

**PhD Researcher** — Empirical Ethics of the Computational
Center for Critical Computational Studies (C³S), Goethe University Frankfurt am Main

## About
Ella's work focuses on NSFW (not-safe-for-work) data and how it travels through platforms, datasets, and machine learning systems. While current research predominantly treats NSFW data as a filtering or moderation issue, Ella is interested in the broader structures behind it, how platforms effectively act as large-scale annotation systems, and how their taxonomies carry over into machine learning and downstream models.

Ella is also interested in the ethics of data processes as they actually unfold in practice, examining how decisions are made during data collection, labelling, and model development, and how ethical considerations can be applied within those processes. She approaches this work through an intersectional lens, informed by feminist, post- and decolonial, as well as queer perspectives.
